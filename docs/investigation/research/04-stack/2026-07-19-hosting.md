# Hosting: VPS vs Fly vs Railway for 4 containers, <= EUR 25/mo — research brief

**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Phase:** 4 (stack & platform fit) · **Author:** research subagent

**Verdict:** A single Hetzner CX23 VPS running Docker Compose with nightly pg_dump offsite lands at ~EUR 8–11/mo all-in, keeps data in the EU, and beats Fly/Railway on cost, privacy, and predictability; Phase 3's EUR 8–13 estimate holds despite Hetzner's June 2026 price hike.

## Findings

- **Workload is lighter than feared.** PowerSync no longer needs MongoDB: Postgres sync-bucket storage since powersync-service 1.3.8; with Postgres 14+, source DB and bucket storage can share one server (docs.powersync.com, 2026-07-19). Current service: v1.23.3, 2026-07-07 (releases.powersync.com). So 4 containers — Postgres, powersync-service, Node API, Caddy — plus a backup cron. Docs state **no minimum CPU/RAM**; 2 vCPU / 4 GB is comfortable single-tenant.
- **Hetzner (recommended).** June 2026 price adjustment (new orders from 2026-06-15): CX23 (2 vCPU/4 GB/40 GB) now EUR 5.49/mo, CAX11 (ARM, same specs) EUR 5.99, CX33 (4/8) EUR 8.49 — all excl. VAT; primary IPv4 EUR 0.50/mo (docs.hetzner.com, 2026-07-19). CX23 + IPv4 + 21% Spanish VAT ≈ **EUR 7.25/mo**; snapshot-backup add-on (+20%) ≈ +EUR 1.30 incl. VAT. FSN/NBG/HEL = EU residency, German company.
- **Netcup (fallback).** VPS 500 G12: 2 vCPU/4 GB/128 GB NVMe at EUR 5.91/mo incl. 19% VAT, 12-month term (hourly billing possible); Nuremberg/Vienna/Amsterdam (netcup.com, accessed 2026-07-19). Cheaper disk, weaker API/tooling than Hetzner.
- **Fly.io.** Pay-as-you-go only (plans discontinued Oct 2024). shared-cpu-1x AMS: ~~$3.19 (512 MB) / $5.70 (1 GB)~~ **corrected (review):** $3.32 / $5.92 — the earlier "fix" pasted Ashburn (iad) rates; volumes $0.15/GB-mo; EU egress $0.02/GB (fly.io/docs/about/pricing, accessed 2026-07-19). Fly's proxy replaces Caddy, so 3 machines: Postgres 1 GB + 10 GB volume, PowerSync 1 GB, API 512 MB ≈ $17–19 ≈ **EUR 15–17/mo** (EUR/USD ≈ 1.14, 2026-07). Under ceiling, but Postgres is unmanaged on a single-host volume, backups stay your job, and Fly is a US company (AMS is physically EU).
- **Railway.** Hobby $5/mo (incl. $5 usage) + usage: RAM $10/GB-mo, CPU $20/vCPU-mo, volumes $0.15/GB-mo, egress $0.05/GB (docs.railway.com, 2026-07-19). ~1.5–2 GB resident + fractional CPU ⇒ **~$20–28 ≈ EUR 17–25/mo** — flirts with the EUR 25 ceiling; consumption billing means a resync loop or memory leak costs money. EU-West (Amsterdam metal) since Feb 2025; US company.
- **Backups.** Cloudflare R2: $0.015/GB-mo, zero egress, 10 GB free tier; Backblaze B2: ~~$0.006/GB-mo~~ **corrected (review):** $6.95/TB ≈ $0.007/GB-mo, free egress to 3x stored (both accessed 2026-07-19). Koi's DB is MBs — nightly pg_dump + optional WAL-G is **~EUR 0/mo**. Hetzner Storage Box BX11 (1 TB, EUR 3.20/mo excl. VAT, Borg/restic/rsync) is nice-to-have overkill.
- **Ops at 0.5 day/month: realistic on the VPS.** unattended-upgrades, monthly pinned-tag container bumps. The failure modes that matter — disk-full and an orphaned logical-replication slot pinning WAL — need one cron alert on disk % + `pg_replication_slots` lag; Uptime Kuma covers uptime. Setup ~1–2 days once. Fly/Railway remove OS chores but **not** the risky ops (WAL slots, backups, PG upgrades stay yours).
- **Phase 3 estimate validated:** EUR 8–13/mo holds on Hetzner/Netcup (EUR 7.3–11.5 incl. VAT with backups); Railway no longer fits, Fly is ~2x.

## Numbers

- Fly.io/Railway: US VC-backed; Railway shipped HA Postgres (Patroni) Mar 2026.

## Risks

- Hetzner may keep repricing; the Compose stack is provider-portable (Netcup, OVH) in an afternoon.
- Solo-dev VPS = you are the pager. Without the disk/WAL-slot alert, a dead PowerSync container can fill the disk and take Postgres down.
- Coolify/Dokploy add the panel as an attack/update surface; plain Compose is fewer moving parts. Dokploy lacks corporate backing (exit plan: it's just Docker underneath).
- Fly reliability reputation and Railway usage-billing variance make both worse fits for a fire-and-forget box.

## Koi fit

Privacy fit and EU residency favor Hetzner/Netcup outright (EU companies, no US CLOUD Act exposure). Invariant preservation and offline depth are hosting-neutral. Solo maintainability: PaaS marginally easier day-to-day, but the hard ops stay regardless; D-022's popularity bar is met by Hetzner + Docker Compose + Caddy. Cost: VPS ~EUR 8–11 vs Fly ~15–17 vs Railway ~17–25. Recommendation: **Hetzner CX23 + Compose (Dokploy optional) + encrypted R2/B2 backups**; revisit if ops load exceeds ~0.5 day/month.
